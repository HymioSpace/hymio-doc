# A small h. A familiar companion.

**HymioSpace is the organization. Hymio Assistant is the product personality.** The lowercase **h** is the organization mark; the blue-scarfed otter is our companion. They have different jobs and can appear together without becoming one symbol.



## The mark: a connection that holds

Two stems, one connection. The bridge interpretation links communication and execution while letting each keep its identity. A small blue gesture brings warmth to a restrained navy structure.

Bridge, Night and Signal above are draft treatments of the same identity, not separate product logos. The navigation retains the current organization mark while these options are explored.

## The otter: capable, curious, considerate

Cream color, clear navy linework, small ears and an asymmetric blue scarf keep the character recognizable. The silhouette should remain legible at small sizes. The scarf can move; the face should remain calm and easy to recognize.

Use the otter to welcome, acknowledge and explain. Use explicit text for status; an expression alone should never be the only indication of failure or completion.

## Voice

- **Direct:** “The comparison is ready.”
- **Honest:** “I couldn’t confirm whether the action completed.”
- **Considerate:** “I need your decision before continuing.”
- **Quiet:** say something when it helps, not merely because a tool ran.

## Palette and motion

Ink navy, warm paper and a deliberate blue accent. Motion follows a meaningful change: accepting work, progressing, reconnecting. The interactive diagram starts only when requested, can be paused, and supports manual stepping. Reduced-motion settings disable its visual transitions.

The brand is an evolving shared vocabulary. Attribution and asset provenance are recorded in the repository's `ASSETS.md`.
